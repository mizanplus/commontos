Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Discord Bot ("Bot") by mizanplus collects, uses, and safeguards information when the Bot is added to your Discord server.

Minimal Data Collection: The Bot collects only the data necessary to function. We do not sell, share, or monetize your data. Server administrators can request full data deletion at any time.

1. Information We Collect

When the Bot is active in your Discord server, it may collect and temporarily process the following types of information:

  • Discord User IDs: Numeric identifiers for users who interact with the Bot.
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs: Identifiers for servers where the Bot is installed.
  • Channel IDs: Identifiers for channels where the Bot operates.
  • Message Content: Only when messages directly invoke Bot commands. The Bot does not log general conversation.
  • Server Configuration: Settings and preferences you configure for the Bot in your server.

We do not collect real names, email addresses, IP addresses, financial information, or other sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Information

Collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Execute Bot commands and deliver requested functionality.
  • Store per-server configuration and preferences.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, spam, and violation of our Terms.
  • Improve Bot performance and fix bugs.

Data is never used for advertising, profiling, or resale.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data to third parties.

Data may only be disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • Hosting Infrastructure: Data is stored on secure hosting providers who are bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Safety: To prevent harm to users or others.

4. Data Retention

Server configuration data is retained as long as the Bot remains in your server. When the Bot is removed from a server, associated configuration data is deleted within 30 days.

You may request immediate deletion of all data associated with your server at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect stored data, including encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security against all possible threats.

6. Your Rights

As a server administrator or user, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about what data the Bot stores for your server.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of all data related to your server or user ID.
  • Opt-out: Remove the Bot from your server at any time to stop all data collection.

To exercise these rights, contact us via the information below.

7. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates on the Discord platform, which has its own Privacy Policy. We may use cloud hosting providers to run the Bot. These providers are selected for their strong security and privacy standards.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced via our support server or documentation. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect when the policy was last revised.
