Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how this bot collects, uses, and safeguards information when operating in your Discord server.

Minimal Data Collection: This bot collects only the data necessary to function. We do not sell or monetize your data. Server administrators can request full data deletion at any time.

1. Information We Collect

When this bot is active in your Discord server, it may collect and temporarily process the following:

  • Discord User IDs: Numeric identifiers for users who interact with the bot.
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs: Identifiers for servers where the bot is installed.
  • Channel IDs: Identifiers for channels where the bot operates.
  • Message Content: Only when messages directly invoke bot commands. General conversation is not logged.
  • Server Configuration: Settings and preferences you configure for the bot.

We do not collect real names, email addresses, IP addresses, financial information, or other sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Information

Collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Execute bot commands and deliver requested functionality.
  • Store per-server configuration and preferences.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, spam, and Terms violations.
  • Improve bot performance and fix bugs.

Data is never used for advertising, profiling, or resale.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data to third parties. Data may only be disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • Hosting Infrastructure: Secure cloud providers bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Safety: To prevent harm to users or others.

4. Data Retention

Server configuration data is retained while the bot remains in your server. When removed, all associated data is deleted within 30 days. You may request immediate deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encryption at rest and in transit, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

As a server administrator or user, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a summary of data the bot holds for your server or user ID.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of all data associated with your server or user ID.
  • Opt-out: Remove the bot from your server at any time to stop all data collection.

7. Third-Party Services

This bot operates on the Discord platform, which has its own Privacy Policy. We may use cloud hosting providers selected for their strong security and privacy standards.

8. Children's Privacy

This bot is not directed at users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The effective date above will always reflect the last revision. Continued use of the bot indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
