Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Facebook Application ("App") by mizanplus accesses and handles your data in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms.

Meta Compliance: This App requests only the Facebook permissions it needs to function. You can review and revoke App permissions at any time via your Facebook App Settings. Revoking permissions will stop all data collection by this App.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect this App with your Facebook account, we may request access to the following data based on the permissions you grant:

  • Public Profile: Your Facebook name and profile picture (public data only).
  • User ID: A unique identifier assigned by Meta for this App.
  • Email Address: Only if you explicitly grant the email permission.
  • Pages / Groups: Only if the App's features require it and you grant access.

We request only the minimum permissions necessary. We will clearly explain why each permission is needed before you grant it.

2. How We Use Information

Data obtained from Facebook is used solely to:

  • Authenticate you and manage your session within the App.
  • Personalize your experience (e.g., display your name).
  • Provide the core features and functionality of the App.
  • Send you important App-related notifications (if you have opted in).

We do not use your Facebook data for advertising, profiling, resale, or any purpose beyond what is described here.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your Facebook data with third parties for their own purposes. Data from the Meta platform is never transferred to advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics firms.

Sharing occurs only in these limited circumstances:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud hosting services operating under data processing agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: When required by applicable law or court order.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as needed to provide the service. If you disconnect the App from your Facebook account via Facebook Settings, or request account deletion, we will delete all associated data within 30 days.

Certain anonymized, aggregated analytics may be retained for longer periods for service improvement purposes.

5. Security

All data transferred between Facebook and our App uses HTTPS encryption. Stored data is encrypted at rest. We implement access controls and conduct regular security reviews to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of all your data from our systems.
  • Revoke Access: Remove App permissions via Facebook App Settings at any time.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

For GDPR and CCPA rights, please submit your request via the contact information below. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Third-Party Services

This App operates on the Meta platform. By using Facebook Login, you are also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review Meta's privacy practices independently.

8. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for users under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as needed to reflect changes in our practices or Meta's requirements. We will update the effective date when changes are made. Continued use of the App after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
